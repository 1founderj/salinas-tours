BEFORE THE CITY WAKES

The Andes are
already waiting.

Long before sunrise, we leave Arequipa behind and climb into the high Andes of the Salinas y Aguada Blanca reserve.

The road opens onto one of the most extraordinary landscapes of southern Peru: an immense white salt lagoon framed by volcanoes. This is more than a transfer — it is a morning built for the senses.

Share a local breakfast surrounded by llamas and alpacas, take in the silence of the salt flats, look for flamingos feeding in the shallow water and enjoy a viewpoint over Pichu Pichu, Misti and Ubinas.

We return to Arequipa at approximately 1:00 p.m., before the highland winds pick up.

04 / THE LANDSCAPE

A white world
above the clouds.

The Laguna de Salinas sits high on the Andean plateau, its white salt crust stretching toward the horizon and changing with the seasons — mirror-flat water in the wet months, cracked salt patterns in the dry.

From the Caccahuasi viewpoint the whole salar opens up beneath you, with the volcanic skyline of Pichu Pichu, Misti and Ubinas watching over the reserve.

08 / BEFORE YOU GOESSENTIAL TRAVEL TIPS

Prepare for
the high Andes.

The lagoon is beautiful, remote and high. A little preparation makes a real difference.

↗

Altitude

Arequipa sits at about 2,300 m; the lagoon rises above 4,300 m. Take a day or two in Arequipa to acclimatise before the tour. Stay hydrated, take it slowly and consult a health professional about altitude sickness prevention when needed.

Coca tea or medication may help, but nothing guarantees you will avoid altitude sickness.

✳

What to wear

Dress in several warm layers. Mornings can be below freezing on the altiplano. Wear comfortable, closed walking shoes. A windproof jacket helps once the sun is up and the wind rises.

☼

Sun protection

High-SPF sunscreen — the Andean sun is strong at altitude. Bring lip balm with sun protection, polarised sunglasses and a hat or cap for the open salt flat.

These tips are general guidance and are not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Is breakfast included?+

What time does the tour start?+

Departure from Arequipa is approximately 5:30–5:45 a.m. The exact pick-up time and point are confirmed when you book.

What should I wear?+

Wear warm layers, comfortable closed walking shoes and a windproof jacket. Bring sun protection for the high-altitude sun.

Can I see flamingos?+

James's, Andean and Chilean flamingos visit the lagoon, but sightings are never guaranteed. They are wild animals in their natural habitat.

Is the tour suitable for people sensitive to high altitude?+

The lagoon is above 4,300 metres. If you are sensitive to altitude or have a medical condition, consult a health professional before travelling and allow time to acclimatise in Arequipa.

Are the hot springs included?+

No. Hot springs entrance is paid separately; the current price is to be confirmed.
